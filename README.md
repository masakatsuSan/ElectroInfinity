Electro Infinity is the shared digital home for an Electrical Engineering student community. It brings the things students usually chase across group chats, notice boards, drives, and personal contacts into one place: learning material, deadlines, routine updates, department news, people, and student work.

## What you can use it for

### As a visitor

Explore the department before signing in:

- Browse courses, faculty, laboratories, placement information, achievements, gallery moments, and student projects.
- Read public announcements and calendar entries.
- Find notes, previous-year questions, assignments, lab manuals, and curated YouTube lectures. Resources can be filtered by semester and subject, previewed, or downloaded.
- Send a message to the student maintainers through the contact page.

### As a student

Activate your account with your roll number and email verification, then use the app as your academic and community workspace:

- Use the dashboard to catch up on recent announcements and discussions.
- Check your batch routine, upcoming assignments, and deadlines; mark an item as submitted when you have completed it.
- Receive and manage notifications so important updates do not disappear in a chat stream.
- Join forum rooms to ask questions, post discussions, comment, and vote on useful replies.
- Discover batchmates, seniors, and faculty; send, accept, or manage connection requests and keep your profile up to date.
- Publish a project with its description, links, images, and tags; browse others' work and show support with likes.
- Maintain a profile that collects your projects, achievements, gallery contributions, social links, and academic identity in one shareable place.

### As faculty

Faculty have a dedicated dashboard for communicating with the classes they teach:

- Post an announcement to a selected batch.
- Categorize messages (for example, academic, class, exam, or urgent) so they are easier to understand at a glance.
- Edit or remove announcements when details change.

### As a class representative or administrator

The administration area keeps the student-maintained information accurate and actionable:

- Publish and manage announcements, events, deadlines, class routines, courses, faculty listings, laboratories, rooms, resources, lectures, projects, gallery items, and achievements.
- Manage student records, including bulk imports and appropriate roles.
- Create forum rooms for focused conversations.
- Review the contact inbox and see an overview of department activity.

## A typical student flow

1. Activate your account, sign in, and complete your profile.
2. Open **Resources** to find material for your current semester and subject.
3. Check **My Workspace** for your routine, new announcements, and assignment deadlines.
4. Ask questions in the **Forum** or connect with people through the directory and network.
5. When you build something worth sharing, add it to **Projects** so the department can learn from it too.

## Access and visibility

Some content is open to everyone, while personal and classroom-specific tools require sign-in. Announcements and deadlines can be targeted to a batch, so students see the updates meant for their classroom. Administrative and faculty controls are available only to the relevant roles.

## The point

Electro Infinity is meant to make student life easier to navigate: less time hunting for the latest PDF or update, more time learning, building, and connecting with the people around you.
